Durango and the Animas Valley (6,500') woke up to light rain and should stay dry through the school run, then another round moves in tonight. I'd expect the 160 into town to be fine for the morning commute.

Bayfield and up the Pine (6,900') same story, a little overnight rain, and I'd expect nothing worse than damp pavement by the 6:30 call. The afternoon's a different deal but that's hours out.

Vallecito and up the Florida (7,650') caught a trace overnight and the 501 and the 240 should be dry for the morning run. Districts decide by 6:30 as always.

The snow line this morning is sitting around 14,000' so this is all rain, even up high in the Weminuche (10,500'). We're not talking about snow at the passes today.

Here's the thing though. The Grand Junction office has us under a Flood Watch through Thursday night, both COZ019 (Vallecito and up) and COZ022 (Durango and Bayfield). They're seeing moisture levels at 300% of normal and calling out the potential for extremely wet conditions Wednesday into Thursday. The NWS discussion mentions that soils are already saturated from the last couple days and there's not much room left for more water. That's the Flood Watch at https://weather.gov/gjt and you should read the whole thing.

The models are lining up on 1.5-2" of rain for most of us between now and Thursday night, with the worst of it coming tomorrow morning through tomorrow evening. Open-Meteo has Vallecito picking up 1.7" total through Thursday, Durango around 2". The Euro, GFS, ICON and GEM are all showing roughly the same setup so there's not much disagreement on this one. Take those totals with a grain of salt (the exact timing of the heaviest bands is still a little uncertain and that'll move the numbers around) but the pattern is solid.

Tonight into tomorrow is when this gets real. The heaviest rain looks like 6am to 6pm tomorrow, and that's when we'll see the highest risk for flash flooding and debris flows, especially in the burn scars and any of the drainages that've already been running high. The 416 scar and Missionary Ridge are both on the list.

This morning's fine. Tomorrow morning is the question.
