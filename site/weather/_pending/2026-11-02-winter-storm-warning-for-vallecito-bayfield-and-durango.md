We're under a Flood Watch through 11am this morning countywide (Grand Junction office). Vallecito and the high country are also under a Winter Storm Warning until noon and an Avalanche Advisory through 4:30pm today. CAIC has the avalanche forecast at https://avalanche.state.co.us/. Rivers across the county are at or near crest right now. The Animas at Durango hit 12,100 cfs overnight. Los Pinos above the lake is running 3,170 cfs. The Piedra's at 12,100. The NWS hydrology discussion says multiple basins are hitting moderate to major flood stage and the worst of it happened overnight, but those levels stay high well after the rain quits.

Do not cross water over a road if you cant see the pavement under it, especially this morning when its still dark out. The districts will make the call by 6:30 but this is a morning to wait for the bus rather than drive if theres any question about creek crossings on your route.

The precipitation's winding down from southwest to northeast through late morning. The models are all over the place on what fell overnight, the Euro has 0.47in for Vallecito, the GFS only 0.1in, the ICON 0.28in, the GEM 0.79in.
